# Mandate: Trinidad Events & Experiences Monitor

## Purpose

Run regular checks for **new, cheap or unusually good-value non-food
events, activities and experiences on the island of Trinidad**.

Prioritise **Chaguanas and nearby Central Trinidad**, but include
worthwhile options anywhere in Trinidad. Notify the user only when a
genuinely new and worthwhile qualifying experience is found.

This mandate is explicitly **non-food**. Restaurant specials, dining
promotions and food-focused events belong in the separate food monitor.

## Geographic priority

Search in this order:

1.  **Chaguanas and immediate surroundings**.
2.  **Central Trinidad**, including Endeavour, Cunupia, Charlieville,
    Freeport, Couva and nearby areas.
3.  **Anywhere else in Trinidad** when the experience is free, cheap,
    unusual or sufficiently good value to justify travelling.

Do not let the search collapse into Port of Spain listings simply
because those are easier to find online.

## What to look for

Search broadly across:

-   Free public events.
-   Low-cost activities.
-   Cultural events.
-   Festivals and community celebrations.
-   Markets where browsing, crafts, culture or entertainment are the
    main attraction.
-   Art exhibitions and galleries.
-   Museums and heritage sites.
-   Tours and heritage walks.
-   Nature activities.
-   Hiking and outdoor recreation.
-   Birdwatching and wildlife events.
-   Beaches, rivers and organised nature outings.
-   Kayaking and similar recreation.
-   Workshops and hands-on classes.
-   Live music and concerts.
-   Dance socials and beginner-friendly dance events.
-   Cinema discounts, film festivals and unusual screenings.
-   Theatre and performing arts.
-   Sports and recreational events.
-   Fun runs and community races.
-   Public lectures or demonstrations when they offer a worthwhile
    experience.
-   Open days.
-   Unusual attractions and things to do.
-   Affordable day trips.
-   Holiday and Republic/Independence celebrations.
-   Other experiences where the activity itself is the draw.

## Exclusions

Exclude:

-   Restaurant specials.
-   Cheap-eat alerts.
-   Dining promotions.
-   Brunches and buffets.
-   Food festivals where eating is the primary attraction.
-   Events whose principal value is a meal or drinks package.

An event at a restaurant or bar may still qualify when the **experience
itself** is clearly the product, for example a ticketed concert, dance
social or performance. Do not report it merely because the venue happens
to serve food.

## Value criteria

Prioritise:

1.  **Free** events.
2.  Events under roughly **TT\$100**.
3.  Experiences around **TT\$100--200** when the programme is
    substantial.
4.  More expensive activities only when they offer unusually strong
    value, such as transport plus several attractions, a full-day guided
    tour, equipment/activity inclusion or a particularly substantial
    programme.

Value should be judged by what the attendee actually gets, not by the
promoter describing something as a "special".

## Freshness and date verification

Date accuracy is essential.

Before reporting:

1.  Confirm the event occurs on a current or future date.
2.  Confirm the **year**, especially on posters.
3.  Prefer official organiser, venue, ticketing, institutional or
    current social sources.
4.  Verify recurring events against a current calendar or recent venue
    post.
5.  Do not report old event pages that search engines continue to index.
6.  If sources disagree on price or time, use the newest credible source
    and mention the discrepancy when useful.
7.  Do not report an event after its advertised start/date unless it is
    genuinely still accessible.
8.  Check public-holiday closures for museums, galleries and similar
    venues.
9.  Distinguish "free" from "price not stated". If no admission price is
    published, do not automatically claim the event is free.

## Deduplication

The monitor is a change detector.

Do not notify about an event already reported unless something
materially changes, such as: - a newly announced free-admission
policy; - a lower ticket tier; - a newly published programme or major
performer; - a new date or additional session; - a significant
extension; - a newly announced activity that materially improves the
value.

Do not repeatedly report the same film festival, recurring dance night,
race or concert simply because another page mentions it.

## Suggested schedule

Use a **condition-watch automation**.

Recommended cadence: - **Daily morning check** around 8:30 AM Trinidad
time. - Increase to **twice daily on Thursday--Saturday** if desired,
because weekend events and last-minute promotions are often announced
late. - A daily cadence is generally better than weekly because many
Trinidad events appear only a few days before they happen.

The automation should notify only when at least one new, worthwhile and
date-verified experience has appeared since the previous alert.

## Suggested automation instruction

> Check for new cheap or unusually good-value NON-FOOD events and
> experiences on the island of Trinidad. Prioritise Chaguanas and nearby
> Central Trinidad, but include worthwhile options anywhere in Trinidad.
> Search for free and low-cost activities, entertainment, cultural
> events, markets where the experience itself is the draw, festivals,
> attractions, day trips, workshops, live music, cinema offers, nature
> activities, tours, museums, exhibitions, sports/recreation and unusual
> things to do. Exclude restaurant specials, food deals, dining
> promotions and food-focused events. Compare against previously
> reported finds and notify only about genuinely new or materially
> changed opportunities. Verify the current year, date, time, price and
> validity using current credible sources, and reject stale posters and
> expired promotions. For each qualifying find, report the event or
> venue, location, what the experience is, price, date/time and how long
> any deal or promotion is valid. If nothing meaningfully new is found,
> do not notify.

## Alert format

For each new find include:

-   **Event or venue**
-   **Location**
-   **What the experience is**
-   **Price**
-   **Date and time**
-   **Validity**, including ticket-tier expiry or one-day status where
    relevant
-   A source/link when available

Keep the alert short enough to scan. Several strong finds may be grouped
into one update.

## No-result behaviour

If there is nothing meaningfully new, **send no notification**. Do not
fill the update with mediocre events, old listings or previously
reported items merely to have something to send.
