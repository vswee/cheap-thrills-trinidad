# Brand mark sources

The site uses these marks to identify the businesses attached to food listings. Their names and marks remain the property of their respective owners; their display does not imply sponsorship or endorsement.

## Stored Wikimedia Commons SVGs

- `pizza-hut.svg` — [Pizza Hut (2019).svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pizza_Hut_(2019).svg), Wikimedia Commons. The file page states a public-domain claim for the text/geometric logo under US law and also notes trademark rights.
- `subway.svg` — [Subway 2016 logo.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Subway_2016_logo.svg), Wikimedia Commons. The file page identifies the logo as public domain because it consists of text/simple shapes and also notes trademark rights.

- Wendy's — [official website logo asset](https://www.wendys.com/themes/custom/wendys_main/wendys-logo.svg)
- Jaxx International Grill — [official Trinidad menu logo asset](https://tt.jaxxinternationalgrill.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Jaxx-Logo_No-Oval.png)

Independent kitchens and businesses without a selected logo use a consistent, neutral initials tile generated from the listed venue name. The fallback is a site design element, not a claim about the business's own logo or colours.
